Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the project and how soon we have your information (text, photos, menu, etc.). A simple site can be ready in a few days to a couple of weeks. I'll give you an estimate once I know what you need.

Is a custom domain included?

No. The initial price does not include registering or renewing a domain (for example, mybusiness.com); that is paid separately. In the meantime, your site can be published for free at a GitHub Pages address, and we can connect your domain later if you decide to buy one.

Do I have to pay monthly maintenance?

No, it's not required. It's an optional service at $200–$400 MXN per month for small changes and updates. If you don't sign up for it, future changes are quoted separately.

Can I update my website myself?

Your site is built with code (HTML and CSS), so changing text or prices means editing files. If you'd rather not, the maintenance service covers it. And if you're interested, I can show you how to make simple changes.
